# Supported disc set

Supply your own original USA Final Fantasy VII discs. The three serials are
SCUS-94163, SCUS-94164 and SCUS-94165. The existing verified per-disc identities
are retained in `disc_set.json` and `game.toml`.

Select the images in the launcher, or place each CUE/BIN pair under `disc/`:

- `Final Fantasy VII (USA) (Disc 1).cue`
- `Final Fantasy VII (USA) (Disc 2).cue`
- `Final Fantasy VII (USA) (Disc 3).cue`

Keep each CUE beside its referenced BIN. Automatic Disc Switching uses the
configured three-disc roster and the game's actual request; manual selection
remains available. See [Automatic disc switching](docs/AUTO_DISC.md).
