Final Fantasy VII @VERSION@

Open Final_Fantasy_7.exe and select your own supported USA game images.
The supported set has disc serials SCUS-94163, SCUS-94164 and SCUS-94165.
You may place the three CUE/BIN pairs under disc/ using the names in DISC.md.
Game images are not included. OpenBIOS is bundled.

Internal Resolution is enabled at 4x by default. Mods offers Native (1x)
through 32x and remembers your selection. GPU and memory limits may reduce
the effective scale. Disabling this mod uses native resolution.

Automatic Disc Switching is enabled by default; configure all three images
to use it. You can also select a disc manually. Native memory cards live
under saves/. Preserve your existing cards and settings when updating.

The existing experimental Widescreen option remains available in Mods.
